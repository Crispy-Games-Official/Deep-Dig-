
# ⛏️ Deep Dig

**Dig deeper. Discover rare ores. Upgrade your gear. Survive the underground.**

Deep Dig is a 2D mining simulator where you explore a massive underground world filled with valuable ores, dangerous monsters, hidden caves, and rare discoveries. Mine your way through the depths, earn coins, upgrade your equipment, and become the ultimate miner!

## 🎮 Features

- ⛏️ **Mining System** — Break blocks, collect ores, and dig your way underground.
- 🌎 **Massive Underground World** — Explore a large, generated world filled with different layers, caves, and hidden resources.
- 💎 **Rare Ores** — Discover increasingly valuable ores the deeper you go.
- 🗡️ **Combat System** — Fight underground monsters using your weapons.
- 👾 **Monster AI** — Enemies chase players, walk around, and jump over blocks.
- 🎒 **Backpack System** — Collect ores while managing your limited inventory space.
- 🪙 **Coins & Upgrades** — Sell your progress for coins and purchase permanent upgrades.
- 🪓 **Pickaxe Progression** — Unlock better pickaxes to mine tougher blocks and reach new depths.
- 🍀 **Luck Upgrades** — Improve your chances of discovering rare resources.
- 🧪 **Potions** — Use special boosts to improve your mining adventures.
- 🏗️ **Building System** — Place blocks and create structures underground.
- 💾 **Save System** — Save your progress and continue your adventure later.
- ⚙️ **Admin Tools** — Access special development tools for testing and managing the game.

## 🕹️ Gameplay

1. Start your mining adventure.
2. Dig through blocks to discover ores and resources.
3. Collect valuable materials in your backpack.
4. Watch out for dangerous monsters lurking underground.
5. Earn coins and upgrade your pickaxe, luck, and other abilities.
6. Explore deeper layers to find rarer and more valuable ores.
7. Build, explore, upgrade, and keep pushing toward the bottom!

## 🎯 Goal

Become the ultimate miner by exploring the deepest parts of the world, discovering the rarest ores, defeating underground monsters, and upgrading your equipment.

## 🖥️ How to Play

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the game's `index.html` file in a modern web browser.
3. Start playing!

No installation should be necessary if the game is built as a standalone HTML file. If the project uses additional local files or browser features that require a local server, follow the setup instructions provided in the repository.
